Our Story

Chicago knows steak.

For more than a century, this city set the standard for American beef. Windy City Wagyu carries that standard forward, with Australian wagyu that is graded by bloodline, scored for marbling and finished on grain for at least 450 days.

01 · The city

The city that fed a nation

On Christmas Day 1865, Chicago's Union Stock Yards opened their gates. For the next 106 years, cattle arrived by rail from across the country, and the city became the center of American beef.

By the turn of the century, the Yards produced 82% of the meat Americans ate. Chicago learned to judge cattle, to grade beef and to cook a steak properly, and it never forgot.

The Yards closed in 1971. The stone gate on Exchange Avenue still stands as a National Historic Landmark, and Chicago is still one of America's great steak cities.

02 · Why we started

Wagyu deserved a clearer label

Wagyu is the most celebrated beef in the world, and also one of the most confusing to buy. The same word can describe a 50% cross or a 100% fullblood animal. Grades are quoted on different scales. Too often, buyers are asked to take the price on faith.

We started Windy City Wagyu to change that. We source Australian wagyu with registered Japanese bloodlines, grade every cut on Australia's official marble score, and print the bloodline and the score on every label.

03 · What we believe

Four promises behind every cut

I

Bloodline first

Genetics decide how far marbling can go, so we sort every animal by its bloodline before anything else.

II

Graded, not guessed

Every carcass is scored on the AUS-MEAT marble score. The number on the label is earned.

III

Patience pays

Every animal spends 450 days or more on grain, and our Black tier spends more than 500. Marbling cannot be rushed.

IV

Nothing hidden

Every animal is traced from birth, with registered pedigrees and no added hormones. You will always know what you are paying for.

04 · The journey

From Australian pasture to your table

Six stages, each one checked.

  1. 01

    Born and tagged

    Registered wagyu bloodlines, given a national ID tag on the farm of birth.

  2. 02

    Raised on pasture

    Early life on open Australian grassland, with no hormone growth promotants.

  3. 03

    450+ days on grain

    A long, patient finish that lets the marbling fully develop.

  4. 04

    Graded

    Each carcass is scored on the AUS-MEAT marble score and assigned to Black, Gold or White by its bloodline.

  5. 05

    Chilled and sealed

    Vacuum-sealed and shipped chilled [never frozen: to confirm].

  6. 06

    Your table

    With the bloodline and the score on the label.
