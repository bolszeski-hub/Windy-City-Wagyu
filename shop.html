Cooking wagyu

Four rules for a perfect cook

  1. 01

    Temper it

    Take the steak out of the fridge 30 to 45 minutes before cooking so it cooks evenly.

  2. 02

    Keep it simple

    Salt is all you need. The marbling does the rest, so skip the heavy marinade.

  3. 03

    Sear it hot

    Use a heavy pan over high heat. The steak releases its own fat, so add little or no oil.

  4. 04

    Size it to the score

    Higher scores are richer. Slice Black thin and share it; enjoy White and Gold as a full steak.
