Cooking wagyu

Four rules for a perfect cook

  1. 01

    Take the chill off

    Take the steak out of the fridge 30 to 45 minutes before cooking so it cooks evenly.

  2. 02

    Keep it simple

    Salt is all you need. The marbling does the rest, so skip heavy marinades.

  3. 03

    Sear it hot

    Use a heavy pan over high heat. Wagyu releases its own fat, so you need little or no oil.

  4. 04

    Match the portion to the score

    The higher the score, the richer the steak. Slice Black thin and share it; enjoy White and Gold as a full steak.
